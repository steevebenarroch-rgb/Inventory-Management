Welcome to PharmIntel

Your pharmacy inventory intelligence tool. Export two reports from your EMS and PharmIntel will surface non-movers, slow movers, overstock, and ordering recommendations.

Step 1
Export current stock

From your EMS, export the current on-hand quantities for all active medications as a CSV or Excel file.

Step 2
Export dispensing history

Export the dispensing history for the last 90 days (or whatever period is available). Include product name or NDC and quantity.

Step 3
Upload both files

Use the Import page to upload each file. PharmIntel auto-detects column names from most EMS formats.

Step 4
Review your analysis

See which items are non-movers, overstocked, or need reordering — with specific quantity recommendations.
